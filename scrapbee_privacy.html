Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-13

This Privacy Policy applies to ScrapBee (the "Extension"), developed and provided by Victor Fence ("we", "us", or "our"). We respect your privacy, and this policy explains how the Extension handles information related to you.

1. Information We Collect

The Extension does not collect, store, or transmit your personal information.

Specifically:

  • The Extension does not collect your name, email address, account information, browsing history, input content, or any other personally identifiable information.
  • The Extension does not send any user data to our servers or to any third-party servers.
  • The Extension contains no analytics, advertising, or user-tracking components.

2. Local File Access

The ability to access local files does not mean personal information is collected; all processing stays on your device.

The Extension includes a local backend component (running via the browser's native messaging mechanism) that has the ability to access local files. We make the following explicit statements:

  • The local backend runs only when necessary to provide the Extension's core functionality, and accesses local files only when triggered by an explicit action from the user.
  • The contents of accessed files are processed solely on your local device to complete the functionality you requested.
  • File contents are never uploaded, transmitted, stored, or shared with the developer, our servers, or any third party.
  • The Extension does not scan, enumerate, or read files in the background that you have not explicitly specified.
  • The backend is open source. Its source code is available at https://github.com/vctfence/scrapbee_backend for inspection and self-compilation.

3. Data Storage

Configuration information for the Extension (such as user preferences) is stored only in your local browser, or handled through the browser's official synchronization mechanism. We have no access to this data.

4. Third-Party Sharing

Because the Extension does not collect any user data, it does not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties.

5. Children's Privacy

The Extension does not collect any personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

If the Extension's functionality changes in a way that affects how data is handled, we will update this policy and revise the "Last updated" date at the top. We encourage you to review it periodically.
